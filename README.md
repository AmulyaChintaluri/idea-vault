# Idea Vault

I built Idea Vault because I wanted one place to capture writing and art
ideas as they came to me. I send an idea to Telegram, and the workflow
turns it into a structured entry in Google Sheets.

Where an idea goes depends on what I want to do with it. A draft for
Substack goes to Self Publish, an idea for a publication goes to Pitch
Tracker, and a painting I want to make goes to Art Originals.

## Example

**Telegram message:** “Substack idea: Why I keep planning projects
instead of starting them.”

**Google Sheets entry:** “Why I keep planning projects instead of
starting them” → Self Publish
